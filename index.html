About this checklist

The VIBES AI Checklist has 16 items in five domains: Validity, Integrity, Bias, Evidence, and Safety. Initial expert-panel content-validity findings apply to the Version 1 item statements. Four items were subsequently revised for Version 2.1, and the revised wording has not been re-rated. This interactive presentation is not independently validated.
